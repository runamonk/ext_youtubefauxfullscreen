Remembered creators

With automatic liking enabled, manual likes remember creators. Their videos are liked after 60 seconds of playback, Shorts after 25 seconds. Shorter Shorts qualify after watching 90%. Existing YouTube like history is not imported.

Select creators, then remove them to stop automatic likes. Turning the toggle off keeps this list. The list stays on this device and is not included in configuration backups.

Appearance

Automatic follows the light or dark preference reported by your browser and updates when it changes. Custom Edge theme colors and images are not copied.

Configuration backup

Export your visibility, playback and theme settings to JSON. Import replaces your settings; missing options use defaults and unknown options are ignored.
